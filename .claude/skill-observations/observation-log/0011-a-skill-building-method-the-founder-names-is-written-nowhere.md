**Issue:** The founder asked for a skill built "by my method": find the
competencies that separate the best practitioners of a craft, then
write the training for each. The session searched the repo, its memory
and the installed skills and found no description of it, so it worked
from that one sentence: three research passes by slice with source
tags, a file-by-file comparison of the candidate bundles, one card per
group of competencies (what the best do, rules, procedure, gate,
drill), and an exam by a fresh subagent.

**Suggested improvement:** Write the method down once, as a short skill
or a section of `docs/`, with the founder's own steps where they differ
from what this session inferred. The brand-designer bundle
(`references/sources.md` "Method", `evals/README.md`) is the worked
example to check it against.

**Principle:** A method asked for by name is a dependency; one that
lives in a founder's head gets re-derived, a little differently, by
each session.
