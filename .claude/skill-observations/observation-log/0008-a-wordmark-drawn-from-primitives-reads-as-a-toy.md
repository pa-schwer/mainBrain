**Issue:** The skill's construction guide says to build letters as paths
from primitives on a grid. A session that follows it to the letter draws
a rounded monoline, and a founder who names Notion and Apple as
references rejects the result on sight: it reads as a toy brand. Two
rounds went that way before a real typeface was outlined.

**Suggested improvement:** The addendum names the floor: an OFL face from
`google/fonts`, outlined with fontTools, shaped with HarfBuzz, then one
letter customised in the kit. The fast track (no questions) stays for a
founder who is not in the chat; one who is gets the five questions first.

**Principle:** Craft the reader can see comes before the idea they have
to read; a concept drawn below the category's floor is judged on the
drawing.
