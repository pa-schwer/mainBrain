## Founder rule

"When I ask for work, you have everything you need to work safely: you
open the PRs, you merge, you deliver. No useless clicks for me. I only
validate staging to prod."

So: never ask before opening or merging a PR into `main`. Never ask before
pushing a feature branch. The one human gate is the promotion of `main` to
`prod`.
