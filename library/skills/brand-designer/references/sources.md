# Sources

The cards paraphrase. No passage is reproduced. A rule in a card
carries one of these tags:

- `[fetched]`: the page was opened while the card was researched
  (November 2026) and the claim is on it.
- `[secondary]`: the claim came through a summary or a report of the
  source. Open the source before quoting it to a founder.
- `[anecdote]`: one person's account of another's practice.
- `[unverified]`: recalled, not confirmed. Treat as a lead.
- `[house rule]` and `[house start value]`: a rule or a number of this
  skill, with no outside authority. The eye decides.

The two engines tag their own cards `[strong]`, `[limited]`,
`[practice]` and `[popular-unsupported]`; those tags stay as upstream
wrote them.

## Method

The founder's way of building a skill: find the competencies that
separate the best practitioners of a craft from the competent ones,
then write the training for each, with a gate that says when the work
reaches that level. Three research passes covered strategy and concept,
mark and type craft, colour, system and delivery. A fourth compared the
two engines file by file. `evals/README.md` holds the exam.
